Power BI Stakeholder Dashboard

Interactive KPI & Performance Intelligence Solution

📌 Project Overview

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to help stakeholders track KPIs, performance trends, and operational metrics without relying on manual spreadsheets or static reports.

🚨 Business Problem

  • Static reports slowed down executive decision-making
  • Manual analysis created inconsistencies across teams
  • Difficult to compare before/after performance
  • Limited ability to segment and drill into data

🛠 Tools & Technologies

  • Power BI
  • DAX
  • Excel
  • Data Modeling
  • KPI Design & Visualization

⚙️ Technical Approach

1️⃣ Data Modeling

Structured and modeled the dataset to enable scalable KPI tracking and consistent reporting logic across business metrics.

2️⃣ Dashboard Design

Designed a clean, executive-friendly layout using KPI cards, trend charts, and performance visuals to highlight insights quickly.

3️⃣ Interactivity & Exploration

Implemented slicers, filters, and drill-down capabilities to allow stakeholders to segment performance dynamically.

4️⃣ Storytelling & Usability

Focused on clear labeling, consistent formatting, and intuitive navigation to support fast executive-level interpretation.

📊 Results & Impact

  • Reduced manual spreadsheet workload
  • Improved reporting speed and consistency
  • Enabled real-time KPI monitoring
  • Enhanced executive visibility into trends and performance

🎯 My Contribution

Led dashboard architecture and KPI framework design, ensuring data consistency and stakeholder-aligned storytelling.
